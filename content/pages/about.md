My creative path began with exploring the fluid interplay of light, hue, and emotional resonance through watercolor painting at NAFA (Nanyang Academy of Fine Arts). Driven by a lifelong reverence for botanical forms, I later spent a transformative year in Tokyo alongside my aging dog, immersing myself in the intentional discipline of traditional Ikebana mastery.

I believe spaces shouldn't feel like sterile showrooms. Today, through **The Seed Atelier**, I synthesize watercolor aesthetics, botanical structure, and spatial curation into Warm Maximalism, a philosophy that celebrates rich textures, vibrant color layering, and lived-in soul. I transform cold concrete interiors into warm, breathing sanctuaries filled with light, nature, and personal narrative.

## Our Design Philosophy: Warm Maximalism

Warm Maximalism is not about clutter, it is about **curated depth and emotional comfort**. It's the art of harmonizing vivid hues, organic fabrics, art gallery walls, and lush greenery into a cohesive, soul-nourishing environment.

By weaving together light, shadow, and nature-inspired palettes, we create spaces that feel deeply personal, inviting, and alive, offering a serene retreat from the fast-paced world outside.
